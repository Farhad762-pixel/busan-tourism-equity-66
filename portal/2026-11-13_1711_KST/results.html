RQ1 and real-world geography

What the other data indicate

Spatial concentration / archived data

Tourism card spending is uneven

Archived 2022–2025 district summaries report Haeundae-gu 29.89% and Busanjin-gu 26.89% of *covered foreign-card tourism consumption*. Provider coverage and raw-processing definitions still need review.

Accessibility / archived data

Straight-line radius is not travel time

Archived 15-km radius experiments report +10.56 to +11.93 percentage-point exposure gains around three approximate origins, but do not measure subway routes or travel duration.

Scientific boundary: these findings characterize a controlled offline recommender, not actual platform outputs or changes in spending. Read measurement and method cautions.
