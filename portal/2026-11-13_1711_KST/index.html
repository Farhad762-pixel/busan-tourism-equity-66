The research question

Can a recommender share attention more widely?

Problem

Tourism attention is uneven

Different districts have different estimated foreign-visitor activity and recorded foreign-card tourism spending. We test how a controlled attraction recommender distributes *simulated visibility*.

Intervention

Balance relevance with opportunity

A transparent economic-undercapture proxy can change a ranking without greatly changing its own word-based relevance score. It does not prove real business benefits.

What these findings do not show

They do not measure new tourist visits, actual spending transfers or the behavior of Naver, Kakao or Google. The human ratings are a small pilot, not population-level validation.
