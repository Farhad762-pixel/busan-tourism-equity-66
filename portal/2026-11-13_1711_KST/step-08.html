Two independent volunteers judge search relevance

Completed a small blinded two-person pilot with 61 unique attraction–search items.

GitHub evidence published: 13 Nov 2026 · 13:41 KST · Korea Standard Time

Why this step mattered

Check whether the ranking's TF-IDF similarity score corresponds to people's judgment of how well attractions match a tourist search.

What was completed

  • Both volunteers reviewed 61 item-query pairs, with 52 jointly rated 1–5.
  • Computed quadratic-weighted agreement κ = 0.490 and exact agreement 34.6%.
  • Found particularly weak matches for local-market and photography-walk queries.

What this does NOT establish
