# Step 08 — Two-person exploratory human relevance pilot

**GitHub evidence timestamp:** 2026-11-13 13:41:02 KST
**Original commit:** [d27d652a3c](https://github.com/Farhad762-pixel/busan-tourism-equity-66/commit/d27d652a3c7e05720030b2547e6dc0896ed7e564) · full commit SHA: d27d652a3c7e05720030b2547e6dc0896ed7e564
**Status:** Completed pilot; stronger validation needed

## Why this step mattered
Check whether word-matching similarity corresponds to people's judgments for selected tourist queries.

## What was done
- Two volunteers rated 61 search–attraction pairs in a six-search blinded pilot; 52 had two valid ratings.
- Recorded exact agreement 34.6% and quadratic-weighted kappa 0.490.
- Identified weak relevance for local-market and photography-walk requests, especially where descriptions were vague.

## What this does NOT establish
Reviewer-level data intentionally remain private. Three fully judged searches cannot prove population-level relevance preservation or non-inferiority. Seek professor's ethics advice.
