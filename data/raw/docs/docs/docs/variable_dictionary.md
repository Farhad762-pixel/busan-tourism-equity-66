# Variable Dictionary

## district
Busan administrative district.

## month
Observation month in YYYY-MM format.

## foreign_visitors
Estimated foreign tourism activity in a district.

## foreign_spending
Foreign-tourist tourism-related spending.

## visitor_share
District foreign visitors divided by total Busan foreign visitors.

## spending_share
District foreign-tourist spending divided by total Busan foreign-tourist
spending.

## recommendation_count
Number of times a destination or district appears in recommendation outputs.

## recommendation_share
Share of all recommendations received by a destination or district.

## recommendation_rank
Position of the POI in the recommendation list.

## algorithmic_visibility_score
Weighted measure of how frequently and how highly a location is recommended.

## poi_count
Number of tourism attractions in a district.

## local_business_count
Number of relevant restaurants, cafes, shops, or tourism businesses.

## latitude
POI latitude.

## longitude
POI longitude.

## travel_distance
Distance between tourist origin/current POI and recommended destination.

## travel_time
Estimated travel time between locations.

## relevance_score
How closely a recommended POI matches the tourist's preferences.

## economic_exposure_need
Measure indicating relatively low tourism economic exposure while retaining
suitable tourism assets.

## geographic_coverage
Number of different districts or areas represented in recommendations.

## exposure_concentration
Degree to which recommendations are concentrated in a small number of areas.
