# Research Plan

## Research Topic

Algorithmic Visibility and Tourism Spending Inequality:
An Economic-Equity-Aware Recommendation Framework for Busan

## Core Problem

Foreign tourism spending and visitor activity may be concentrated in a
small number of Busan tourism districts.

Digital recommendation systems may reproduce this concentration by
repeatedly recommending already-popular destinations.

## Research Goal

Determine whether recommendation exposure is associated with existing
foreign-tourist spending concentration.

Then test whether a re-ranking model can provide more exposure to suitable
tourism locations in lower-benefit areas without substantially harming
recommendation quality or travel convenience.

## Study Area

Busan Metropolitan City, South Korea.

## Geographic Unit

Main analysis:

Busan district (gu/gun) level.

Possible second analysis:

individual tourism POI / business level.

## Research Stages

### Stage 1
Measure foreign-tourist spending concentration.

### Stage 2
Measure foreign visitor concentration.

### Stage 3
Build the Busan tourism POI database.

### Stage 4
Measure recommendation exposure.

### Stage 5
Compare recommendation exposure with spending and visitor concentration.

### Stage 6
Build baseline recommendation model.

### Stage 7
Build economic-equity-aware re-ranking model.

### Stage 8
Compare models using relevance, geographic coverage, exposure concentration,
and travel convenience.

### Stage 9
Interpret business and tourism-policy implications.
