2. Research Problem

Foreign tourism spending is concentrated in certain Busan districts. Recommendation systems may reinforce unequal visibility by repeatedly prioritizing already prominent destinations.

This study develops and evaluates an economic-equity-aware recommendation model to investigate whether suitable attractions in lower-benefit districts can receive more visibility.

9. Main Research Contribution

The study provides an experimental framework showing how district-level economic information can be used to adjust tourism recommendation rankings while maintaining high measured text relevance.

The results demonstrate simulated recommendation visibility changes, not proven improvements in local economic outcomes.
